What We're All About

We specialize in high-altitude, artisanal single-origin coffee beans, subscriptions, and custom blend crafting for coffee purists.

The Velocity Roast Story

We started Velocity Roast Co. with a simple goal: bring micro-lot, high-altitude coffee directly from mountain farms to your cup. Coffee grown at high elevations (4,000+ feet) grows slower, producing denser beans packed with vibrant, complex flavors. We source exclusively from sustainable, fair-trade farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, Sumatra, and Guatemala.

Our Craft & Process

Small-Batch Precision
We don't mass-produce. Every order is small-batch roasted right after it's placed to lock in peak freshness.
Roasted to Order
Your bag is stamped with the exact roast date before it ships out, guaranteeing you never get stale store-shelf coffee.
Tailored for Purists
Whether you brew espresso, French press, or pour-over, we match the roast profile and precision grind to your exact brewing setup.

Why Custom Blending Matters

Off-the-shelf coffee forces you to compromise on taste. By letting customers choose their origin, roast profile, and grind type, everyone gets a bag crafted specifically for their palate.
